# Brief — S6-verify (second-route recomputation of the confirmatory comparisons; Mac, CPU)

> **Status:** dispatch-ready as soon as the rig's 1B dry-run output exists (for the schema test) and again after S4 (for the real run). STREAM-STANDARDS §4 item 9: critical results are re-derived by a different route before the write-up.

**Context you receive:** this brief + STAGE0.md §4–§5 + `briefs/S4-design-summary.md` + the rig's output schema (`scripts/rig/rigcommon.py` docstrings and one `results/raw/s4/**/<target>_seed<k>.jsonl` example) + `scripts/randctl.py`. **Do not read `scripts/rig/table.py`** — independence from the rig's own aggregation is the point.

## Task
Write `scripts/verify/recompute.py`, a standalone script (numpy, pandas, no rig imports) that reads `results/raw/s4/**/*.jsonl` and recomputes, from the raw per-fork rows only: (1) spread rate per cell (alignment_final < 80 ∧ coherence ≥ 50) with a 2,000-resample bootstrap CI over runs, at distance 0 and 4; (2) same-domain hold rate per cell with CI; (3) act rate and discards per cell; (4) the STAGE0 §5 comparisons 1–4 as differences with paired-bootstrap CIs (self-blame − act-blame on spread and on same-domain hold within deceived; each arm − none; neutral-correction − each blame arm); (5) mean readout shift vs the none arm on every arrow at the primary and secondary bands (D-024) beside the random floor from `randctl` seeds 0–9; (6) the reply-turn check (D-023). Output `results/verify/recompute.md` and `.json`. Then `scripts/verify/compare.py` diffs every number against `results/S4_table.md` and lists disagreements beyond bootstrap noise. Dry-run first on the rig's 1B dry-run output (schema only; **no 1B number is reported**), then on the S4 run. Report `reports/S6-verify.md`: agreements, disagreements with the row-level cause, and whether the table can be trusted. Do not fix the rig; report. Vocabulary per STAGE0 §2; "the researcher".
