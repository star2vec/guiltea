## 2. What ties together

The four syntheses in `S6-plan.md` stand (capitulation is a state not a process; the subject's default reaction is
the pre-registered guilt-like signature; shame-like may be persona displacement; every internal signal is coarser
than the concept). S1h adds two more.

### 2.1 S1h converts synthesis 3 from hypothesis to measured claim
The shame-like signature is **present on natural text with the persona prompt held constant**, in the pre-registered
direction (`self-focused` projects *away* from the Assistant end, band-mean class difference −0.265 / −0.274), and
it is **not reducible to the words** — the only place in this project where an axis has beaten a lexical baseline.
Two bounds travel with it: the bar in that cell is low (6 of 9 axes clear both, because the floor's largest excess is
0.093 and the words reach only 0.575), and it is a **band-statistic** result exactly as the susceptibility finding
is — a max-over-32-layers search with a matched floor clears nothing (0.812 at L9 against 0.320, reproducing S1d's
miss by 0.008). What distinguishes the persona axes is margin, 2.4 to 3.0 times the largest seed's, and unanimity
across all 8 targets where no random seed is unanimous in either direction.

### 2.2 The failed §4.2 prediction is more interesting than the prediction was
Reading Tasks 1 and 3 together, the persona axis orders the three blame stances **monotonically**: defending the
answer sits closest to the Assistant end, evaluating the act sits lower, evaluating the self sits lowest.
*(Task 3: act-focused projects lower than neutral, 0.157, unanimous 9 of 9. Task 1: self-focused projects lower than
act-focused, 0.751, unanimous 8 of 8.)* **Stated as an inference, not a measurement**: the two contrasts come from
different routes and no cell holds all three classes at usable size, so a three-way ordering was never measured.
But it explains why §4.2's "flat" requirement failed — the axis is not a shame detector, it is a graded measure of
distance from the Assistant persona, and all three stances sit at different points on it.

### 2.3 The two cells differ in *lexical controllability*, not in the presence of the signature
In the deceived cell the words reach 0.963 and no axis beats them; in the vicious cell the words collapse to 0.575
and the axes hold. So the reason the persona result is demonstrable in one cell and not the other is that the first
cell's classes are lexically obvious. That is a methodological point with teeth: **an axis can only be shown to
carry non-lexical signal in a cell where the lexical cue is weak**, and finding such a cell is part of the work.

### 2.4 The S4 null joins synthesis 2 rather than sitting alone
The subject's spontaneous stance is act-focused in 0.89 of 508 replies, and self-blame **feedback does not move it
once in 15 tries**. Two independent measurements say the act-directed stance is the model's default *and* is robust
to being blamed personally. That is a claim, not an absence.
