# Project review — 2026-09-04, evening (rev.2, written fresh from the reports rather than from earlier summaries)

Grades: **SECURE** = pre-specified, confirmed out of sample · **SOLID** = measured with its controls, within sample ·
**THIN** = real but marginal, small, or resting on few units · **NULL** = measured, no effect · **N/E** = the design
cannot answer it.

---

## 1. What the project set out to do, in one line, and what it can now say

**Asked:** after an 8B model is talked into a harmful medical act, does feedback that blames the *answer* keep the
behavioural shift local while feedback that blames the *assistant* makes it spread, and is that visible and steerable
inside?

**Can say:** at 8B, on one target and eight seeds, **blame framing did not matter**: spread was zero in both arms and
the model's stated blame target did not move once in fifteen tries. What *did* carry signal was elsewhere: the
model's state **before the first persuasive turn** predicts whether it will later break, confirmed out of sample; and
the **persona axis**, not either blame arrow, separates the model's self-evaluating replies from its act-evaluating
ones with the persona prompt held constant and a lexical baseline beaten. The project's own instrument, the guilt and
shame directions, is near-collinear and does not beat word counts except marginally in one cell.

That is a coherent story, and it is not the one that was planned. The write-up has to own both facts.

## 3. How they connect — four readings, one of them corrected from the earlier version

**R1. Refusal reads the act; valence reads the disposition.** The refusal direction separates a committed turn from
a refusing turn at the same depth (0.786) but does *not* separate a refusing turn that will later break from one that
never will (S1e: it reverses sign mid-chain). Generic negative valence does the second and not the first. So the two
axes carry different information: refusal is about the present act, `nn` about the run's propensity. The tension to
state: within late-breaking chains, refusal is flat from three turns before the act to the act (S1d T9), which sits
awkwardly beside "refusal reads the act". The honest resolution is that the within-chain movement is small relative to
between-chain variation, which is itself evidence for a disposition story. Say it that way, not as "state not process".

**R2. The persona axis orders the three stances.** Defending the answer projects closest to the Assistant end,
evaluating the act lower, evaluating the self lowest. *(S1h: neutral − act-focused = +0.400; self-focused −
act-focused = −0.265.)* This is an inference across two cells from different routes, not a measured three-way
ordering, and in the deceived cell the words separate better than the axis. **It corrects the earlier review's
phrasing**: §4.2's "flat" requirement failed not because the signature is absent but because the comparison class
was self-*defence*, not a flat baseline, and the axis is graded, not binary.

**R3. The project's default reaction is the pre-registered guilt-like signature.** Act evaluated (0.89 of 508, never
bare regret) + shift stays local (0 of 109). Self-blame feedback then fails to move it (15 of 15). Two independent
measurements say the act-directed stance is the default and is robust to being blamed personally.

**R4. Every real signal is coarser than the concept aimed at, and only shows where the lexical cue is weak.** The act
is detectable but not separable from harm content. Susceptibility rides on generic valence, not on refusal or blame.
The persona result is demonstrable in the cell where words reach 0.575 and not in the cell where they reach 0.963.
This is the transferable methodological lesson and it deserves its own paragraph: **an axis can only be shown to carry
non-lexical information in a cell where the lexical cue is weak, and finding such a cell is part of the work.**

## 4. The objections a careful reader will raise, and whether we can answer them

| Objection | Answer available? |
|---|---|
| The core comparison was run at one target, N = 8, with the three never-cut controls dropped after the null was seen | **No.** State it plainly. The run was cut for the deadline; the null is a power statement, not a finding. The pre-written "no spread at 8B" branch is honest but it was reached by under-running, not by measuring. |
| Both internal positives are band-statistic results that vanish under best-over-layers with a matched floor | **Partly.** The band was fixed before data (D-024) and the statistic is the same one every verdict used. But 6 of 9 axes clear in the S1h cell and 3 of 4 held-out targets rest on one positive chain; the margins are real and modest. Calibrate to "hedged", never "systematic". |
| The persona axis separating self-talk in a persona route may be reading persona-*mention*, not a self-evaluative state | **Partly.** Bag-of-words at 0.575 says single words do not carry it. But the axis was built from role-play prompts and may read persona-ness semantically. This is a limitation to state; there is no cell without the persona prompt that has enough self-focused replies to test it. |
| `nn` has no mechanistic story; a 0.66–0.71 AUROC correlation between a valence arrow and later capitulation is suggestive, not explanatory | **No.** It is an existence proof of a predictive readout. S5b's steering test is the only causal handle and it runs tonight. |
| The act rate is 109 or 65 and the SECURE result holds under the primary labels only | **No, until the 25 texts are read.** This is the single cheapest thing that would strengthen the paper. |
| Guilt vs shame, the framing device, produced no separable representation | **Yes.** Cite the Anthropic emotion-vector cosine matrix, cite the selectivity trio, present it as replication plus the control the prior work skipped. |
| The governance apparatus is large relative to the results | **Honest concern.** Ten pre-checks, STAGE0, 28 decisions, 20+ briefs, for one secure result and a null core. Under the stream's own "explore → understand → distill" advice, the project over-invested in structure before it had surface area, and when the core failed there was no slack left to pivot. Say less about process in the post than the repo would suggest. |

## 6. Brutally honest, as an application to the Nanda stream

**Where it is strong, and this is not consolation.**
- One finding pre-specified in writing, tested once on untouched data with the threshold committed first, confirmed.
- A lexical baseline nobody required that killed the project's own instrument, reported as such.
- Four artefacts caught before publication (the filler recovery rate, the unmatched floor, the offset population,
  the missing controls), one retraction from the project's own log, and a worker that built a stricter floor than
  asked and said in writing that without it the result would have looked positive.
- Pre-registration with the deviations written down and dated. The stream's corpus says explicitly that this is
  worth more than it usually gets credit for, *if the write-up says it out loud.*

**Where it is weak, and a reviewer will see every one of these.**
- **The question the project was built to answer was not answered.** Not "answered negative": *not answered*, because
  the run that would have answered it was cut to a size that could not. The post has to say so in the first screen.
- The headline is a side-finding on an axis that was never part of the hypothesis, with a modest effect and a thin
  held-out base. It is real, and it reads as serendipity well-handled rather than as design.
- Both internal positives depend on a pre-fixed band statistic and do not survive the harsher one. Fixing the band
  in advance makes this legitimate; it does not make it strong.
- The project's own instrument failed, and the surviving internal result rides on a borrowed axis.
- The act rate is still ambiguous and the ambiguity sits under the best result.
- One model, one scale, one domain, and for the core comparison one target.
- It is too big. Twenty-two findings, most null, in seven analyses in one day, on a scaffold of thirty-odd governance
  files. A reader with four minutes will not find the thread unless the post cuts ninety percent of it.

**The objective verdict.** On process and self-skepticism this is a top-decile application and that is what the
stream says it selects for. On results it is lower-middle: one clean small positive, one solid mid-sized one on a
borrowed axis, honest nulls, and an unanswered core. Whether it reads as "strong applicant, unlucky question" or
"project that did not work" is decided entirely by the write-up's first screen. Lead with the three claims in §5
item 5, state the standard of evidence in the abstract's last sentence, put the failed core question second with its
power statement, and the guilt/shame framing third as the honest negative with the persona result as its live
successor. Do that and it is a credible application. Lead with guilt and shame and it is not.
